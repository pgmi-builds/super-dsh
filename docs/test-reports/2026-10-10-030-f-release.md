- PC proxy reads, PC direct reads without proxy, authenticated reads and DEV3 reads all returned404 for `super-dsh/0.3.0-f`. Both hosts' whole packuments omitted F and retained `latest=0.3.0-e`; the direct tarball URL was also404. This rules out the tested local npm cache and proxy-only visibility explanations.

These observations do not identify a scan finding and do not justify changing policy metadata or the approved archive.

The [official npm discussion](https://github.com/orgs/community/discussions/203413) describes work on scan-status visibility and maintenance notifications for long delays. The [status page](https://status.npmjs.org/) was operational with no October9 incident, which does not resolve an individual package's processing state.

**Established boundary:** the approved Super archive was submitted and accepted by the official registry; public visibility remains unresolved inside registry processing.
